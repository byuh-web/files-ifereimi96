First Card

In the United terminal in Chicago at five on a Friday afternoon The sky is breaking with rain and wind and all the flights Are delayed forever. We will never get to where we are going And there's no way back to where we've been.The sun and the moon have disappeared to an island far from anywhere. .

Second Card

In the terminal of stopped time I went unsteady to the beat, Driven by a hungry spirit who is drunk with words and songs. What can I do? I have to take care of it. The famished spirit eats fire, poetry, and rain; it only wants love. .
