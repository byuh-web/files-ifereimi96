Why I Chose This Topic

I chose Book of Mormon heroes because their stories teach me faith, courage, obedience, and trust in Jesus Christ.

Nephi

Nephi showed faith by following the Lord even when it was hard.

Alma

Alma teaches repentance and how people can change through Christ.

Captain Moroni

Moroni was brave and defended his family, faith, and freedom.

Ammon

Ammon served others and helped people come closer to God.

Mormon

Mormon protected sacred records and stayed faithful in hard times.

King Benjamin

King Benjamin taught that when we serve others, we serve God.
